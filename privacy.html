This website is operated by Praesidio Health LLC ("Praesidio Health," "we," "us"), a physician-led healthcare management services organization based in West Orange, New Jersey. This policy describes how we handle information in connection with this website.

This website does not ask you to create an account, fill out forms, or submit personal information, and it does not use its own cookies or advertising trackers. Health information

This website does not collect, store, or transmit medical or health information, and nothing you do on this site creates a patient relationship with Praesidio Health or any medical practice. If you become a patient of an independently owned practice we support, such as Atlantic Spine Center, your health information is held by that practice under its own privacy practices and applicable law, including HIPAA where it applies. Please do not include medical details in emails sent through links on this site.

How we use and share information

We use the limited information described above to operate this website and respond to inquiries. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing purposes.
